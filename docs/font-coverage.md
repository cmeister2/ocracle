# Printed typography coverage

The target is every typeface previously printed on Magic cards, including italic text. That requirement remains open. This inventory records verified historical names, printed treatments that need separate examples, and missing evidence. A set code, font-family name, or successful title crop does not establish coverage of every face, weight, text field, or language. Sources checked 2026-10-19.

Latin-script languages are the priority; other printed alphabets are best effort. See [the language inventory](language-coverage.md). Phyrexian recognition is an optional stretch goal.

The frozen [corpus summary](../training/corpus/summary.json) records 126 acquired printings of 120 card identities: 6,902 lines, including 230 real scan crops and 74 italic scan crops. Actual scan crops represent 110 print IDs and 105 card names. The nine scan cohorts are `lea`, `7ed`, `8ed`, `tsp`, `m15`, `m21`, `fut`, `plc`, and `mp2`; they are sampling cohorts, not nine distinct fonts. There are 179 real scan lines in training, 36 in validation, and 15 in test. Counts describe data, not recognition accuracy.

The designer's [Beleren style specimen](https://delvefonts.com/static/img/beleren-2.png) names six cuts: Bold, Bold Italic, Bold Small Caps, Bold Italic Small Caps, Heavy Small Caps, and Wide. The remaining family members and each cut's printed-card usage still need evidence. The [project cover specimen](https://delvefonts.com/static/img/beleren-0.png) also records an expansion in 2016, so a single M15 title is insufficient evidence for the entire family history.

Closing the full requirement requires an inventory of released printings grouped by text field, script, treatment, and evidenced font/cut. Unidentified lettering must remain visible as a gap. Add manually checked printed transcriptions, including punctuation and a defined mana/tap-symbol representation, rather than substituting Oracle text. Reserve independent card identities for testing and report clean, italic, and multiple blur levels separately for each group, including failures and sample counts.

The original six cohorts' real crops were selected for exact agreement with the base recognizer, which favors easier examples. The 30 `fut`/`plc`/`mp2` titles were added without that filter. A comprehensive benchmark must include difficult, unfiltered examples across all groups. Match evaluation preprocessing and decoding to deployed Rust inference; synthetic substitute fonts and an aggregate score cannot establish recognition of every historical printed font.
